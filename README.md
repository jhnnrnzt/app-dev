## The Godfather

##Summary
**The Godfather**(1972) was a crime drama about how the Corleone family survive the 5 mafia family war in Newyork.
It start when Don Vito Corleone was shot, after refusing the drug trade represented by other family.
His youngest son Michael Corleone who always stayed out to family business, was force to step-in for his family.
The movie emphasize how Michael change from innocence to cold blooded leader of the family.
